# Data Licences

This repository contains software and generated food-data packs.

The software and the third-party data are separate works. A software
licence applied to repository code does not automatically apply to
third-party datasets.

## Open Food Facts country packs

The following generated files are derived from the Open Food Facts
database:

```text
countries/<ISO2>/full.json
countries/<ISO2>/main.json
countries/<ISO2>/fill.json
countries/<ISO2>/manifest.json
```

The Open Food Facts database is available under the Open Data Commons
Open Database License 1.0:

https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Individual database contents are available under the Open Data
Commons Database Contents License 1.0:

https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/

The modified Open Food Facts country packs published by this
repository are made available under ODbL 1.0.

Contains information from Open Food Facts, which is made available
under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Source:

https://world.openfoodfacts.org/data

The packs are provided in machine-readable JSON form in this public
repository.

## Product images

The generated country packs do not contain Open Food Facts product
images.

Product-image licensing is therefore outside the current pack
pipeline.

## Other datasets

National food-composition datasets are kept in separate files and
must retain their own source-specific licences, attribution and
modification notices.
